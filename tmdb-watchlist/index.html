Movie & TV Watchlist (TMDB)

Search for movies and TV shows, save them to a personal watchlist, and later pick something to watch. Everything is stored locally in your browser.

Search TMDB

How to use (and where to get a TMDB key)
  1. Get a TMDB API key from your TMDB API settings.
  2. Paste the key/token below (it’s stored only in this browser).
  3. Search and click Save to watchlist on anything you want to keep.
  4. Later, use Pick something to choose a random unwatched item.

Note: your key is effectively a password—avoid sharing screenshots that include it.

Privacy & data

Your watchlist and credential are stored in localStorage in this browser. Requests are sent directly from your browser to TMDB. Nothing is uploaded anywhere else.
